# ADR-0011: Geofence geometry v1 approved for all three MVP chokepoints

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Source:** CHOKEPOINT-PLAN.md §5.4, §20 step 4; research/geofence-candidates.md; ADR-0007

## Context

ADR-0007 blocked all membership computation until a named review owner approved
real coordinates. Candidate geometry for all six fences was researched from
public sources (per-vertex provenance tags in
`research/geofence-candidates.md`): 33 CFR §110.214 and §167.500–503 for
LA/Long Beach (exact corners), the Suez Canal Authority Rules of Navigation
(2020) for both Suez fences (published points, inferred edges), OSM
seamark:type=anchorage polygons for the Singapore roadstead (ODbL), and an
estimated corridor for the Malacca/Singapore strait (all vertices approximate;
IMO Ships' Routeing is paywalled).

## Decision

The review owner (ChrisSchmidt, GitHub: PCSchmidt) approved **all six candidate
fences as geometry v1** (`geometryVersion: "2026-09-09-v1"`, effective
2026-09-09), explicitly including the known approximate vertices and the
Malacca corridor's ±0.05° uncertainty. The fences are committed to
`src/config/chokepoints.ts` as `geometryStatus: "reviewed"` and registered in
`REVIEWED_GEOFENCE_REGISTRY`. Limitations are carried on each profile and must
remain visible in the product.

## Consequences

- Geofence-bound metrics (vessel_count, entry/exit) are unblocked.
- The Malacca corridor counts carry stated ±0.05° geometric uncertainty; a v2
  re-derivation from IMO Ships' Routeing is the expected future change.
- The Singapore roadstead inherits ODbL attribution; share-alike applies if the
  derived geometry is redistributed.
- The SCA Zone 1 east-limit typo is recorded as treated-as-32°27.0′E pending
  chart verification.
- All fences remain coarse demo geometry: "geofence does not equal official
  port boundary" stays a displayed limitation forever (§4.3).
